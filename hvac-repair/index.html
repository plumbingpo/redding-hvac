Heating and cooling systems rarely fail politely, and in Redding, CA, a breakdown tends to land on the hottest afternoon or the coldest night of the year. The tricky part is how many different failures produce the same symptom. A system that runs but never reaches temperature might be fighting a weak run capacitor, a pitted contactor, a low refrigerant charge, a dirty flame sensor, or simply a filter so clogged the blower is starving for air. A system that will not start at all could be a tripped float switch on a backed-up condensate drain, a failed transformer, a bad thermostat, or a control board that has stopped sequencing the equipment.

HVAC Repair Prevention Tips

A little routine maintenance goes a long way toward avoiding a mid-season breakdown. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Trim shrubs and clear leaves and grass away from the outdoor unit so the coil gets full airflow
  • Schedule professional maintenance twice a year, before cooling season and before heating season
  • Ease the strain in extreme weather by nudging the thermostat a few degrees toward the outdoor temperature
  • Flush the condensate drain line each spring before algae can clog it and trip the float switch
  • Take new noises seriously; a squealing motor bearing is far cheaper before it seizes
  • Swap in a fresh filter every one to three months; a clogged one starves the whole system of air
  • Leave every supply and return vent open and free of furniture or rugs
