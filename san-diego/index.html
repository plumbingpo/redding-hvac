Getting More Years From Your HVAC System in San Diego

Routine maintenance makes all the difference between a system that lasts and one that breaks down in the middle of the season. Our advice for homeowners is a short list: a new filter every one to three months, two feet of clearance around the outdoor condenser, a spring rinse of the condenser coil, and two professional tune-ups a year, so problems are found while they're still inexpensive.

How old is your system? Its age says a lot about what comes next. With regular service, most air conditioners make it to 12 to 15 years, furnaces 15 to 20, and heat pumps 10 to 15. If your system is past that point and also shows higher bills, rooms that never quite reach temperature, or frequent repairs, it's usually due for a planned replacement, which is almost always cheaper and easier on everyone than a rushed emergency install when the first heat wave hits.

The earlier you report a comfort problem, the cheaper it usually is. A capacitor that's starting to fail costs little to replace, yet the same part, neglected through a full season, can ruin the compressor and turn a simple fix into a full system replacement. Save our number on your phone today so you can reach us fast the moment something seems off in your home. A quick call saves time, money, and miserable nights.
