When bills climb, airflow drops, or ice forms on the lines, have the system checked before a repair turns into a replacement.

Maintenance Plans

Maintenance Plans for Redding Homes

Routine maintenance is the most reliable way to keep your home comfortable year-round. Regular service prevents failures before they start, keeps coils and burners clean, and catches small faults while they're still inexpensive to fix. At Redding HVAC, every plan is built around your home's actual equipment, with scheduled visits that cover coil cleaning, refrigerant charge verification, capacitor and contactor testing, burner and heat exchanger inspection, condensate drain clearing, filter replacement, and a static pressure check across the duct system.

We offer flexible maintenance options for homeowners, with spring cooling tune-ups and fall heating tune-ups for most systems. That's especially important in dusty areas, where dirty condenser coils choke heat rejection and drive up energy use. Each home is assessed individually, and our technicians tailor the plan to your equipment's age, your household's comfort demands, and your local climate.

Homeowners are often surprised how much a consistent maintenance schedule cuts operating costs. A clean coil transfers heat far more efficiently, a properly charged system stops overworking the compressor, and a fresh filter protects the blower motor. Most families find that a maintenance plan costs far less than replacing an HVAC system even one year early.

Proper Sizing

The Right Size System for Your Home

When it comes to HVAC, bigger is not better. An oversized unit turns on and off too often, burns extra energy, and never runs long enough to pull humidity out of the air, while an undersized one runs nonstop and still falls behind on the hottest days. That's why we never just copy the tonnage of the old unit. Instead, we run a proper load calculation that factors in your square footage, insulation, windows, and ductwork, and for mini-splits we size each head to the room it serves.

Homes change over time. An addition or a finished basement can overwhelm the single-stage system that used to be plenty. Modern two-stage and variable-speed heat pumps hold tighter temperatures and use much less energy. Whether you're comparing a heat pump and a furnace-and-AC pair, or ducted and ductless, we'll explain the real trade-offs in SEER2 ratings, operating cost, electrical and venting needs, and installation work.

Frequent Failures

Heating and Cooling Problems We Solve in Redding

No cooling, a furnace that refuses to light, ice on the indoor coil, leaking refrigerant: each of these problems has a different cause and needs a different repair. Redding HVAC tracks down the exact fault before replacing anything, whether it's a condenser that hums without starting, a furnace that short-cycles, a thermostat that loses its settings, a clogged condensate drain, or a mini-split throwing error codes.

Some failures can wait a day, and some can't. A bad capacitor just keeps the compressor from starting. A cracked heat exchanger, on the other hand, can leak combustion gases into your living space, and a blocked condensate drain can soak through a ceiling before anyone notices. Our technicians carry the parts to replace capacitors, contactors, fan motors, igniters, flame sensors, and control boards, repair leaky ducts that dump conditioned air into the attic, and recharge refrigerant once a leak is fixed.
