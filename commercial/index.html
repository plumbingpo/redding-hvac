Kitchen Climate Control

Comprehensive Food-Service HVAC Systems

Few buildings work an HVAC system harder than a restaurant. Every burner, fryer, and oven adds heat, the exhaust hoods pull conditioned air out nonstop, and the makeup air system has to replace that air without upsetting the building's pressure. Redding HVAC sizes, repairs, and maintains the rooftop units, split systems, and makeup air equipment that keep the kitchen and dining room comfortable, and we schedule service around your hours.

Many restaurant comfort complaints trace back to air balance. If a kitchen runs under negative pressure, it pulls dining-room air in the wrong direction, sucks hot or cold outdoor air in through every door, and wears out your staff at peak service. Our technicians balance the makeup air to the hood exhaust and log every visit in written reports your landlord and auditors can review.

Your schedule comes first. We coordinate every replacement and maintenance visit with owners, managers, and kitchen staff. Off-hours installation is available when needed, and we keep disruption to a minimum so the kitchen keeps running and the dining room stays comfortable.

Safe Installation

Professional Installation and Documentation

Commercial property owners have a duty of care to maintain safe, comfortable conditions for employees, customers, and tenants. A poorly installed or neglected HVAC system can result in carbon monoxide hazards, poor indoor air quality, refrigerant leaks, and costly water damage from clogged condensate lines. Redding HVAC provides commercial installations and inspections that cover heat exchanger integrity, proper venting and combustion air, electrical connections and disconnects, refrigerant charge verification, condensate drainage protection, and ventilation rates for occupied spaces. Our written reports identify risks, assign priority levels, and recommend specific preventive actions.

Consistent professional service protects your people and your property, and following through on our recommendations helps keep minor issues from becoming emergencies. It also builds a paper trail. We keep service logs, temperature and filter records, and a binder of reports for every account, and we increase visit frequency for heavy-use equipment in kitchens, gyms, and large mechanical rooms. That record shows landlords, insurers, and auditors that you've done your due diligence.
